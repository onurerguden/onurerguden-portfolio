## LinkedIn About — English

I build AI applications that connect retrieval, orchestration and usable product experiences. My work spans Python, Java and TypeScript, with hands-on experience in RAG pipelines, vector search and full-stack development.

I graduated in Software Engineering from İzmir University of Economics in June 2026. At Future Is Now, my work has included client-facing AI applications, LangChain and LangGraph workflows, and Qdrant-based retrieval architectures.

My projects range from Kuyumcum, a mobile precious-metals and savings ecosystem I co-developed, to water-safety classification and forecasting, departmental knowledge retrieval, and urban-mobility analysis. I am particularly interested in AI, machine learning and data science, including how model evaluation translates into reliable applications.

I am a co-author of “Two-layered Artificial Intelligence System to Assess and Forecast the Safety Level of Drinking Water Resources,” accepted by the International Journal of Engineering Approaches (IJEA), with publication pending.
