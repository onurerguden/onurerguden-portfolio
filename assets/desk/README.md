# Onur's desk source

Original model authored for Onur Ergüden from his supplied setup photographs. Photos are local reference only and are not embedded or redistributed. Source screen contents are excluded. The September 7 unobstructed photo controls monitor and riser alignment; the clean wide photo controls the remaining arrangement; September 6 close-ups control riser/drawer, Lenovo foot, Xiaomi clamp/dial, laptop keyboard and headphone/lamp details.

## Rebuild

Use Node 24 and Blender **4.5.13 LTS**. On this machine Blender is installed in `/Users/onurerguden/Applications/Blender.app`.

```sh
/Users/onurerguden/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --python scripts/desk/build.py -- --render
npm run desk:package
npm run test -- tests/desk-model.test.ts
```

The script overwrites only its generated model, camera contract, report and rendered previews. `onur-desk.blend` retains individually named editable geometry, packed authored textures, lights and a review camera. The delivery GLB batches geometry by material and retains named screen anchors. `artifacts.json` records outputs by hash; re-rendering may change byte hashes across graphics drivers or Blender versions.

Official macOS ARM64 installer: https://download.blender.org/release/Blender4.5/blender-4.5.13-macos-arm64.dmg

Verified against the official `blender-4.5.13.sha256`: `663ce944257c61ff1d6aa09e15c8f57bbd8d59023adb2fa7edde33a9ed960b53`.

## Scale and approximation

Metres in Blender (Z up), glTF and camera contract (Y up). Desk dimensions are user-confirmed 1.50 × 0.80 m. Samsung 24-inch portrait, Lenovo 29-inch ultrawide, Xiaomi lightbar, MacBook Pro 14 M1 Pro, Razer Barracuda and MX Master 3S are user-identified. Monitor product numbers and IKEA stand SKU were not supplied: housings and accessory placements are photo-derived approximations, not manufacturer CAD. The revised stand follows the dimensions of the visually matching IKEA BRYTET; see docs/desk-model.md for research and remaining calibration. Display ratios default to 16:9 rotated and 21:9; MacBook housing is based on the identified 14-inch generation.

The IKEA metal riser is separate from the Lenovo silver base. Its top, drawer and sides use an original repeating mesh texture, not thousands of wires. Keyboard legends, device silhouettes, lightbar clamp, both mouse wheels and two rests are geometric. Tablet is decorative and is not a fourth journey stop. The right lamp starts pink and supports five runtime colors; the left light, monitor lightbar and rear bias light share a runtime switch. The September 10 cabinet reference guides the four-drawer right support and matching white slab left support; their dimensions are approximate. The backdrop remains black.

All textures are generated by the script: 128 px material patterns and two 1024 px neutral AO receiver maps, packed in GLB. Receiver maps are filtered at texel scale and JPEG-compressed; the mesh alpha mask stays PNG. No externally licensed device model, image or HDRI is used. Draco decoder is an unchanged, separately licensed dependency in `public/decoders/draco`.

Interactive groups are batched by both identity and material. `src/lib/desk-interactions.json` is generated from the same source and defines stable node names, world pivots and expanded pick volumes. Animated objects are omitted from the receiver bake so their shadows do not remain at rest positions. Regenerate the browser loading poster after runtime lighting changes, then package again; see `docs/qa/desk/README.md`.
